Campus Connect is a centralized web platform designed to help students stay informed and actively involved in campus life.
It allows students to discover upcoming events, club activities, and hackathons, while enabling club heads and administrators to post, manage, and approve events through role-based access.
The platform also supports hackathon team formation, where students can find teammates based on skills and interests within the campus. By bringing students, clubs, and opportunities onto a single platform, Campus Connect improves communication, participation, and overall engagement in campus activities.




1. Systematic Explanation of Campus Connect
1️⃣ Problem Statement

In most colleges, information about events, club activities, and hackathons is scattered across WhatsApp groups, notice boards, and word of mouth. Students often miss opportunities, clubs struggle to reach the right audience, and there is no organized system for hackathon team formation or event management.



2️⃣ Proposed Solution – Campus Connect

Campus Connect is a centralized web platform that brings all campus-related activities into one place. It acts as a digital hub where students, club heads, and administrators can interact through a role-based system.


3️⃣ User Roles and Responsibilities

Student

View upcoming events, club activities, and hackathons
Register for events
Search and join hackathon teams based on skills and interests
Club Head
Create and manage club events
Post hackathons and activities
Track student participation
Administrator
Approve or reject events posted by clubs
Monitor overall campus activity
Maintain platform integrity


4️⃣ Key Features

Centralized event and hackathon listing
Role-based access control (student, club head, admin)
Hackathon team formation within campus
Improved communication between students and clubs
Organized and transparent event management


5️⃣ System Workflow (High Level)

User logs into the platform
Role is identified (student / club head / admin)
Dashboard loads based on role
Events and hackathons are fetched and displayed
Students register or form teams
Club heads manage events
Admin approves and oversees activities


6️⃣ Benefits of the System

Reduces information gaps on campus
Increases student participation
Simplifies event management
Encourages collaboration through hackathons
Creates an active and connected campus ecosystem
