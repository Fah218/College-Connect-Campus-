🎮 HOW TO USE:

1. LOGIN (Demo Mode):
   - Go to http://localhost:3000/login
   - Email: test@test.com (any email works)
   - Password: password123 (any password works)
   - Select role: Student/Club Head/Admin
   - Click "Sign In"

2. REGISTER FOR EVENTS:
   - Login as Student
   - Browse events
   - Click "Register Now"
   - Fill form → Submit

3. FORM HACKATHON TEAMS:
   - Go to Hackathons
   - Click on any hackathon
   - Click "Post Request"
   - Fill skills/roles
   - Other users can apply
   - Accept applicants
